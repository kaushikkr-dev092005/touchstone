## Critique and corrections

1. The initial radial growth read as a mechanical starburst. It was replaced with curved tapering tubes, rounded tips and a downward bias. Reference-like branching remains a visible difference.
2. The initial ore appeared like a flat slab. Plane-derived shape/normals, microfacets and surface shards now break up its silhouette and reflection.
3. The initial wipe looked like bright texture across a flat plane. The body is now dark and mottled, with specular brightness concentrated at the meniscus. The flash expires after about 160ms even when scrolling stops.

The squint check keeps one object and one caption as the focal point. Caption scrims, inherited type and the existing grid preserve readable controls. Mobile framing was corrected after a capture showed excessive cropping; graph positions now account for the sticky stage when resizing. Desktop labels turn inward at the right edge; mobile labels form a compact readable list beneath the graph.
